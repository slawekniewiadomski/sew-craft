# Run: 3D preview of a sewn assembly

Source doc: `.ai/specs/2026-11-07-3d-preview.md`
Decision: `docs/decisions/0011-3d-preview-renderer.md`
Stacked on: `feat/seams-and-sewability` (PR #140), not `develop`.

## Goal

An assembly preview that turns the stitch loops and seam record into a 3D
dummy: triangulate panels, weld the seam sides, inflate the shell along panel
normals, and render flat-shaded triangles as SVG polygons. Read-only, ephemeral
and deterministic; no schema or command change.

## Scope

- Geometry: `drape.ts` — ear clipping, conforming subdivision, seam sampling and
  insertion, welding, the inflate/distance/bending pass, open boundaries and the
  triangle budget.
- Frontend: `lib/preview-mesh.ts` (projection/shading), `PreviewDialog.vue`, the
  `preview` ribbon action and icon, and the `EditorView` wiring.
- Docs: the preview contract in `geometry-core.md`, ADR-0011, and a short note in
  the run/spec assets.
- Evidence: an integration screenshot of the built preview.

## Non-goals

- No WebGL or three.js (ADR-0011), no textures, shadows or smooth normals.
- No gravity, collisions, self-collision or time integration.
- No body collider, no saved camera or pose, no 3D export.
- No document, command-spec or schema change.

## Decisions carried in

- Orthographic SVG, painter's algorithm, flat Lambert shading.
- The solver is pure in `@sewcraft/geometry`; the Vue layer only projects.
- Seam layer order decides inflation direction: side 0 `+1`, others `-1`.
- Seam samples are welded vertices; finished edges stay open; no caps.
- `PREVIEW_SAMPLE_SPACING = 8`, `PREVIEW_MAX_EDGE = 18`,
  `PREVIEW_ITERATIONS = 60`, `PREVIEW_TRIANGLE_LIMIT = 6000`.
- The preview is derived and read-only; the preview action works in read-only.

## Assumptions

- 100 % statements/branches/functions/lines remain mandatory in
  `packages/geometry/src` and `frontend/src`.
- The integration harness and Playwright are available locally; integration does
  not count toward unit coverage.
- The stacked PR targets `feat/seams-and-sewability`; when #140 merges, the
  preview branch rebases onto `develop` and the seam commits disappear.

## Risks to watch

- **Ear clipping on real loops.** Arc-flattened loops and rounded bridges are
  simple but dense; a collinear or repeated point must not hang the ear search.
  Test degenerate inputs and cap the ear scan.
- **Seam arc mapping.** The side polyline must map onto a contiguous loop slice;
  a hand-edited side may not. Fall back to skipping the weld, never throw.
- **Welding two panels changes triangle orientation.** Inflation uses per-panel
  direction, not a global normal, so orientation does not decide the result.
- **Self-intersection.** Accepted; the preview is a dummy. Keep the step small
  enough that a pouch does not tangle at maximum inflate.
- **SVG cost.** 6 000 polygons is already a lot for Vue; the budget must be
  enforced before rendering, and orbit updates should be throttled by Vue's next
  frame rather than a timer loop.
- **Coverage.** The solver is pure and tested; keep WebGL-free DOM code thin and
  test the pure projection separately.
